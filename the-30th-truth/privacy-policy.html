The 30th Truth — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy explains how The 30th Truth, an Android mystery game developed by Mystery Box ("we", "us"), handles information. It is published at https://mysterybox144.github.io/games/the-30th-truth/privacy-policy.html.

The cases play without an account. The game does not ask for access to your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, photos or files. It uses Google Mobile Ads and the Google User Messaging Platform for optional rewarded ads and ad consent, and Google Play for optional in-app updates.

Game information kept on your device

The game saves these records on your device using Android DataStore:

  • which cases you have opened and solved;
  • the leads, scenes and evidence you investigated, and any hidden clues you found;
  • your choices at turning points and in final decisions, and your latest case outcomes;
  • a case in progress, so you can return to it: its stage, the number of mistakes, remaining lives and decisions made so far;
  • your appearance setting (light, dark or system).

The Google User Messaging Platform also stores your ad consent choice on the device. These records refer to prewritten fictional stories. They stay on your device and are not uploaded to any server run by Mystery Box.

Typed answers and fictional keypad codes are used only while you play and are not saved. There are no user accounts, online leaderboards, cloud saves, chat or user-generated content.

Optional rewarded ads and consent

The game uses Google Mobile Ads and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). These services may contact Google to check and manage consent. Where consent and applicable law allow it, a rewarded ad may be loaded before you tap the option to watch it, but an ad is only shown after you choose to watch one. Watching an ad gives one extra life and is always optional: when you run out of lives you can start the case again for free.

Google and its advertising partners may process device and app information, IP address, the advertising ID where available, approximate location derived from the IP address, app interactions (such as launches, taps and ad views), diagnostic data and consent signals. They use this to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and comply with the law. What is processed depends on your device settings, your region and your consent choices. The game never sends your typed answers or keypad codes to advertisers.

Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for teens (Google's "T" maximum ad content rating). For how Google uses data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy. When required in your region, the game shows Ad privacy choices under Settings so you can review or change your consent.

Google Play updates

The game uses Google Play in-app updates. When it checks for, downloads or installs an update, Google Play may process device, installation and diagnostic information under Google's own privacy terms.

Data security

Android app-data backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for game data. Unencrypted network traffic is blocked, so the ad and consent services use encrypted connections.

Retention and deletion

Your game records stay on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the game. Clearing storage resets all progress. No account is created, so there is no account to delete. Google and its advertising partners keep the data they process under their own retention policies, and clearing app storage may not delete data they have already received. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings.

Children and audience

The 30th Truth is meant for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. Its content rating is 12+. Mystery Box does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game in a way that shared their personal information, contact us at the address below and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If the way the game handles information changes, we will update this page and the date at the top before releasing that version of the game.
